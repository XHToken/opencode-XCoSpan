# Code of Conduct

## Community standards

Treat contributors and users with respect. Keep discussions focused on the work, explain disagreements with evidence, and welcome constructive feedback. Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and accessibility needs.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized remarks, personal attacks, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable. These standards apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.

## Reporting

To request a private reporting channel, open an issue titled **Private conduct report** without incident details or identifying information. Share the report through the private channel supplied by a maintainer. Include relevant links and the outcome you are seeking. Keep personal information and sensitive evidence out of public discussions.

For software vulnerabilities, follow [Security](SECURITY.md).

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers review reports, consider relevant evidence, protect reporter privacy, and explain moderation decisions to affected participants where appropriate. A maintainer involved in a report should leave its review to another maintainer.

Responses may include a request to change behavior, removal of harmful content, or a temporary or permanent restriction on participation, according to the severity and recurrence of the conduct.
